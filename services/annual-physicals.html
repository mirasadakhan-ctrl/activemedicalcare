Annual physicals in Fairfax, Virginia

Plan a preventive visit with Dr. Nisa Masood in Fairfax, VA. Care in four languages. Call (703) 620-6221 to schedule.

An annual physical is a chance to discuss your health before a small concern becomes harder to manage. At Active Medical Care in Fairfax, Dr. Nisa Masood, MD, provides preventive visits with attention to your history, questions and daily life. The right examination, tests and follow-up plan depend on the individual; a visit does not mean everyone needs the same screening package.

What to expect

Your visit begins with a conversation about your medical and family history, current medicines, allergies and any changes since your last appointment. Bring questions about sleep, activity, nutrition or other concerns that matter to you. The doctor may check vital signs and perform an examination suited to your needs. You can also discuss which vaccinations and age-appropriate screenings are due. Lab work or referrals may be recommended when appropriate, rather than automatically ordered for everyone. Before leaving, ask what the next steps are, how results will be communicated and when to return. If you need a form completed, tell the office when booking so the visit can be planned appropriately.

Who it's for

This service is for people seeking preventive primary care, establishing care with a new doctor or reviewing their ongoing health needs. Your doctor can advise how often a preventive visit makes sense for your age, medical history and risk factors. Mention any new symptoms when scheduling; a focused problem visit may be more appropriate than a routine physical.

Preparing for your visit

Bring your insurance card, photo ID, medication list and available records from other doctors. Include nonprescription medicines, vitamins and supplements. A short written list of your priorities can help you use the appointment well. Ask the office whether you need any preparation for planned tests; do not fast or stop medicines unless your care team tells you to. Please arrive early enough to complete any requested paperwork.

Frequently asked questions

Is an annual physical the same as a Medicare wellness visit?

No. A Medicare Annual Wellness Visit focuses on a personalized prevention plan and is not a routine physical examination. Ask which appointment and coverage apply to you.

Will everyone have the same lab tests?

No. Testing depends on your history, symptoms and screening needs. Discuss the reason for each recommended test with the doctor.

Can I bring a list of questions?

Yes. Bring your main questions and tell the office about forms or additional concerns when scheduling.

Care is available in English, Hindi, Urdu and Telugu.

All insurance plans accepted. Please call to confirm your coverage before your visit.
